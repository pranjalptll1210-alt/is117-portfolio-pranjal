## 2. Two Portfolio Approaches

### Approach A — Project-First Portfolio

Show my best projects immediately after a short introduction. Each project will explain its purpose, my contribution, the tools used, and supporting evidence.

**Advantage:** Recruiters can quickly see what I have worked on.

### Approach B — Résumé-First Portfolio

Start with my education, professional experience, skills, and résumé before introducing projects.

**Advantage:** Recruiters can quickly review my background.

### My Decision

I prefer Approach A because my projects demonstrate my technical skills and problem-solving abilities. I will still include my résumé, education, LinkedIn, and GitHub profile.
